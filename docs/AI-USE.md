# AI use

**Human-directed research. AI-assisted execution. Reproducible evidence. Human
responsibility.**

This study was a working partnership between Roseworth Research and an AI
assistant, Claude (Anthropic). Claude was used extensively as a research,
software-engineering, analytical and drafting tool, under the direction and
review of Roseworth Research. Neither side could have produced the study alone.

## What Roseworth Research did

- **Originated and directed the research.** Roseworth identified the question,
  set the scope, chose the sources and decided what the study would and would
  not collect.
- **Set the standards.** The evidential standards, the confidentiality rules (no
  client data, no person-level data), the publication criteria and the
  commitment to publish results whatever they showed were Roseworth's.
- **Made the judgements.** Which hypotheses to test, which findings to lead
  with, which claims the evidence could carry, how sensitive subjects are
  described, and every editorial and publication decision — including the
  decision to report findings that ran against the study's own starting
  hypothesis.
- **Reviewed the work.** Roseworth reviewed the analysis and drafts throughout,
  commissioned and answered the independent written reviews, and directed every
  correction.
- **Takes responsibility.** Roseworth Limited is responsible for the
  publication and for any errors.

## What Claude assisted with

- Software development: the data pipeline in `pipeline/`.
- Analytical-method exploration and implementation, and the statistical
  analysis.
- Primary-source research, including the VAT review in `docs/vat-review/`.
- Drafting and typesetting of the papers.

## How the results are grounded

Claims in the papers derive from identified public data, executable code,
reconciliation procedures and documented review — not from unsupported model
output.

- Every response from the public registers is cached, so every figure can be
  recomputed from the same observation.
- Headline figures were recomputed from the database by an independent route.
- The method and statistics were examined in three independent written reviews,
  each answered point by point and adopted.
- An independently constructed second classifier re-derived a sample of the exit
  classifications.
- Every population total was reconciled to its parts.
- Every correction is recorded in [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md).

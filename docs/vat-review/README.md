# VAT review

The VAT treatment behind Paper 2 and section 9 of Paper 1, checked question by
question against primary sources on 1 October 2026: legislation.gov.uk (VAT Act
1994, Schedule 8 Group 1), HMRC VAT Notices 700, 700/1, 700/12, 701/14, 706,
709/1 and 742A, the HMRC VAT Food manual, court judgments, filed company
accounts and the delivery platforms' published terms.

Every answer gives a short extract, the citation and URL, and the consequence for
the model. One answer changed the model (D1: cold ready-to-drink coffee is
zero-rated, moving Costa from £8.51 to £8.55 per £10). Three points no public
source settles — coffee pods, Just Eat's restaurant terms, Greggs' current
kept-hot share — are labelled as such and affect no printed figure.

This is general information about the structure of the rules, not advice on any
business's VAT position.
